# Jet 317: Curaçao–Panama Home score and playback hang

Private Jet iPad only, 12 November 2026 Tobago. These are separate checks: a correct score does not establish playable media, and a responsive player does not establish a successful broadcast.

- Before the repair, Jet 316 showed Curaçao–Panama on PP1 with a black Buffering screen. On two Watch attempts, AVFoundation rejected PP1 before first picture. One request showed a 502 in the relay window; another showed a 200 in that window. Those receipts do not establish that the same segment failed and recovered.
- At 21:53:47 and 21:57:01 Tobago iPadOS reported 0x8BADF00D (failed to terminate gracefully). Both main-thread stacks waited in the native time-control callback while the relay rewrite held its state lock and synchronously persisted a quality event. Foundation's defaults observer waited on the main thread. This explains the app freeze independently of why the media failed.
- A manifest rewrite now stores only a Boolean in the relay's transaction. The ordinary diagnostic snapshot copies it under the lock; serialization/persistence happens outside the lock. No changes to which media destinations, signatures, or headers are accepted. The regression replays an actual-shaped opaque segment through the loopback relay and checks it rewrites successfully without any synchronous quality-store event.
- Captured ESPN men's Concacaf Nations League fixture 401929776 at 02:02:32 UTC on 13 November (22:02:32 Tobago on the 12th): Curaçao home 0, Panama away 0, second half 73′. In the deployed pre-repair fixture the status was live but footballLive was absent. The repaired collector, durable registry and exported snapshot retained 0–0 and 73′. On Jet the private DEBUG Home replay of that exact receipt showed 0–0 / 73′. No receipt was restamped; replay is disabled in Release.
- A separate normal-catalog Watch on the private candidate discovered CS1 and PP1. CS1 timed out and Fotty automatically moved to PP1. Neither produced a verified picture. The player exited buffering, displayed Retry and Sources, and responded to a tap by opening a sheet listing CS1 and PP1. This check verifies control recovery, not playback of the match.
- On that repaired PP1 retry, Jet recorded the same CoreMedia -12927 rejection and `relay_unaliased_segment=true`, followed by a terminal failed Watch outcome at 02:09:49 UTC. The media request window included HTTP 200. This is positive evidence that the previously blocking branch actually ran; it still does not identify the cause of the upstream/decoder incompatibility.
- Normal signed Release 317 was then installed with the existing app container. Its 02:13 UTC Home Watch returned PP1 as the only candidate. AVFoundation reported the same -12927 twice, and the app exited retry at 02:14:09 UTC. Normal Release showed the failure/Retry screen and allowed closing it without a relaunch.
