### Precise failure attribution

Preparation502 here is **Fotty's local relay response**, not proof of upstream HTTP502. `LocalStreamProxy.forwardRequest` catches `invalid_response`/URL−1011 and emits502; actual upstream non-success statuses take a separate `http_status` branch. The rejected response/final destination/unfollowed redirect and a URLSession response failure are collapsed at this boundary. Actual upstream status and exact cause remain unknown. A future bounded failure-origin/redirect-decision enum would distinguish them without recording URLs or credentials. Expired account/access, a provider placeholder and redirect incompatibility are hypotheses, not established causes.

The live result therefore does not close Highfly programme qualification or establish the historical rugby/basketball cause. It does validate that the two confirmed app integrity defects are repaired, local tests pass, and normal Watch can fall back to the correct live Spain programme. Belgium failure is retained, not hidden by that success.
